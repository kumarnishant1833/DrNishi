Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions we receive from our patients.

While walk-in consultations are welcome during clinic hours (10 AM – 7 PM), we strongly recommend booking an appointment via WhatsApp to avoid waiting time and ensure dedicated attention from Dr. Nishi Yadav.

The number of sessions varies depending on your skin type, condition, and treatment goals. Generally, laser treatments require 4–8 sessions spaced 4–6 weeks apart. Dr. Nishi will give you a personalised treatment plan during your initial consultation.

Yes, PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) therapy is a clinically proven, safe procedure that uses your body's own blood plasma to stimulate hair follicles. It is non-surgical, minimally invasive and has minimal downtime. Most patients see visible improvement after 3–4 sessions.

The most effective approach for acne scars typically combines chemical peels, micro-needling, and laser resurfacing. The ideal combination depends on the type and severity of your scars. Dr. Nishi will assess your skin personally and recommend the best course of action.

Most treatments involve minimal discomfort. Topical anaesthetic creams are applied before laser and injectable procedures to maximise your comfort. Our team ensures a gentle, reassuring experience throughout your visit.

Results vary by treatment. Laser pigmentation results can last 1–3 years with proper sun protection. Chemical peel benefits last 3–6 months. PRP hair results generally last 12–18 months. Dr. Nishi will advise on maintenance treatments to sustain optimal results.

Please contact us directly via WhatsApp or phone to inquire about consultation and treatment fees. We strive to offer affordable, transparent pricing with no hidden costs. Detailed pricing is provided at your initial consultation.

Yes! We offer comprehensive pre-bridal skin care packages designed to give you your best skin for your wedding day. We recommend starting at least 3–6 months before the big day. The package includes facials, peels, brightening treatments, and personalised home-care guidance.
